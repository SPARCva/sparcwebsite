Upload photos

Drag in photos (or click to choose). Capture dates are read from each photo automatically so the header scroll stays in order through the day. Add a caption and the names of people in the photo, then upload.

Import from Google Photos, Google Drive, OneDrive & Facebook

Add from Google Photos: pick photos in Google's own picker (from any album, including ones shared with you) and they import into the album & year selected above. Capture dates come across automatically.

Import by link: paste one or more publicly-viewable image URLs (one per line) and they'll be copied into this album. Works for any public image URL.

Auto-sync (next step): automatic OneDrive "Summit Photos" + Facebook album sync, and a scheduled Google Drive folder sync, can be wired up once the matching API credentials are added. See supabase/functions/photo-gallery/README.md.

Manage photos

Select photos to tag several at once, mark which appear in the header scroll, change the year, or remove them. Click a photo to edit its caption and people.

People & faces

Free, private face recognition that runs entirely in your browser — no accounts, and no photos are ever sent to another company. First scan the photos to find faces, then name each person once; every matching photo is tagged automatically and becomes searchable by that name on the site.
