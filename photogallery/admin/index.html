Upload photos

Drag in photos (or click to choose). Capture dates are read from each photo automatically so the header scroll stays in order through the day. Add a caption and the names of people in the photo, then upload.

Drop photos here or click to choose  ·  JPG / PNG / WEBP, up to 25 MB each
Import from Google Photos, Google Drive, OneDrive & Facebook

Import by link (works today): paste one or more publicly-viewable image URLs (one per line) and they'll be copied into this album. Great for Google Photos "share link" images or any public image URL.

One-click pickers & auto-sync (next step): a Google Photos / Google Drive picker button and automatic OneDrive "Summit Photos" + Facebook album sync can be wired up once the matching API app credentials are added. See supabase/functions/photo-gallery/README.md for the exact setup, and Claude can also bulk-import directly from a shared Google Drive folder on request.

Manage photos

Select photos to tag several at once, mark which appear in the header scroll, change the year, or remove them. Click a photo to edit its caption and people.
