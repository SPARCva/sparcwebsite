Donate an Auction Item

Support adults with severe and multiple disabilities at An Evening to SPARCle

Your item helps build a rightful place in the community

An Evening to SPARCle is SPARC's signature annual celebration, and the silent auction is one of the evening's most important sources of support. Every dollar raised goes to work in our centers — 1:1 support from trusted Direct Support Professionals, community outings, art and music therapy, and the adaptive tools that make participation possible for adults with severe and multiple disabilities.

If you own a business, represent one, or simply have an experience worth bidding on, we would be grateful for your donation. Gift certificates, tickets, getaways, services, tastings, rounds of golf, jewelry, artwork and unique experiences all bid especially well with our guests.

Please submit your donation by Tuesday, October 20, 2026, so that we can include your business in the gala program and our printed materials.

About the event

  • An Evening to SPARCle — SPARC's annual gala
  • Saturday, November 14, 2026, 4 PM – 8 PM
  • Washington Dulles Airport Marriott
    45020 Aviation Drive, Dulles, VA 20166
  • Benefiting SPARC — Specially Adapted Resource Centers, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit (EIN 20-5513060)
  • Donation deadline: Tuesday, October 20, 2026

How we thank our donors

  • Recognition in the gala program
  • Your logo on this page and in our annual report
  • Recognition across SPARC's social media
  • Visibility with our guests and online visitors
  • An in-kind gift receipt for your records

Sending your item, logo and photos

Please email a company logo (a .jpg for social media and a vector file for banners), photos of the donated item, and any advertising materials to debi@sparcsolutions.org.

Physical items may be mailed to SPARC at 1775 Tysons Blvd., Fifth Floor, Tysons, VA 22102, or we are glad to arrange a pickup — just tell us on the form which you prefer.

Questions about the auction? Email debi@sparcsolutions.org or call (571) 407-1807.
